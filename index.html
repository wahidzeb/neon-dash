About Neon Dash

Neon Dash is a one-button runner set in a glowing synthwave city. Your cube runs on its own and you only decide when to jump.

There are 200 levels and 10 music tracks, and levels never change, so you improve run by run. A yellow marker on the progress bar shows your best run, and coins buy cube skins in the Skin Shop.

3 quick tips

  1. Jump a little before the spike, not on it.
  2. Hold on runs of spikes, tap for single obstacles.
  3. Learn one section at a time, and watch the yellow best marker to know when you reach new ground.

How to Play

Your cube dashes forward by itself. Tap the screen (or press Space / ↑ / W) to jump.
Clear the pink spikes and purple blocks. Touch one and you crash. You can't stand on blocks.
Grab coins in the air, and get +5 for every level you finish. Spend them on 44 neon skins.
Reach the finish gate to unlock the next level. There are 200 levels, and they get faster and denser as you go. The yellow mark on the bar is your best run.
Pause with ⏸, Esc or P. R restarts. The game pauses itself if you switch tabs.

Tip: turn your phone sideways to see further ahead.
